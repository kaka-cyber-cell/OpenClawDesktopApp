Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 09, 2026

1. Information We Collect

OpenClawDesktopApp is a local application. By design, we do not collect or store your API keys, chat records, or personal configurations on any cloud servers. All configuration data (like Feishu App IDs or DeepSeek keys) are stored locally on your machine in the `.env` or `openclaw.json` files.

2. Locally Processed Data

Any data you provide via our GUI is processed purely to facilitate the operation of the OpenClaw AI engine on your local computer. This includes your communication with Feishu, WhatsApp, or Telegram.

3. Third-Party Services

Our software connects directly to third-party APIs (OpenAI, DeepSeek, Google, etc.) based on your configuration. Please refer to their respective privacy policies regarding how they handle data sent from your machine.

4. Updates

We may update this policy occasionally. Continued use of the software constitutes your agreement to the updated policy.
